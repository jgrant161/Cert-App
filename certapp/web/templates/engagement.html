Every certificate's seller must be this client: {{ e.seller_name or e.client_name }}. Shortened forms pass ("Additive Manuf" for Additive Manufacturing LLC); a different company fails and the certificate is marked Invalid; a misspelling or a longer name is sent to a reviewer to confirm.

Form acceptance

A Streamlined (SST) certificate is flagged Invalid for any state that is not an SST member (23 full members plus Tennessee as an associate, as of October 2026). Some non-member states do accept it: use a reviewer override for those. Your tax team keeps the Uniform list below current.

Expiration rules when a certificate states no date

One state per line: a number of years from the signature date (IL: 3 years), December 31 of the year signed (FL: calendar year), or no expiration (TX: none). A date printed on the certificate always wins. Your tax team confirms each state's rule before relying on it.
