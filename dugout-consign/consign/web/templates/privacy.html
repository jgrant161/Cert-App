Privacy

This portal is run by {{ setting("shop_name") }} (Harrison’s Cards and Collectibles LLC) to manage items consigned to the shop.

What we keep

For consignors: name, contact details, payout details, the items you consign with their photos, prices, sales and payouts. For staff: name, email and sign-in history. We keep a log of changes so every sale and payout can be traced.

When an item sells on eBay, we record the order number, sale price and fee. We do not store eBay buyers' names, addresses or other personal information.

How it is used

Only to sell your items, pay you, and keep accurate records. We do not sell or share your information. Item photos and descriptions are published on the marketplaces where your items are listed.

Your choices

Ask the shop at any time to correct your details or to close your account. Sales and payout records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.
